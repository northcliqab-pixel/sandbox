01 About

Who NorthCliq AB serves and how the business enters the market.

The company sits at the intersection of product supply and commercial sourcing. The message should stay simple: NorthCliq AB supports business buyers looking for dependable access to arts, craft, and textile categories with a direct, professional, trade-oriented relationship.

Company Snapshot

NorthCliq AB is a new B2B-focused organization shaping a commercial presence around two core tracks: arts and craft products and textile commodities. The aim is to create a trusted entry point for wholesale buyers, sourcing conversations, and long-term trade relationships from Göteborg, Sweden.

Commercial Focus

  • B2B arts and craft product distribution
  • B2B textile commodity sourcing and supply
  • Relationship-led growth with buyers and partners
